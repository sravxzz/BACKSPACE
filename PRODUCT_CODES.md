# BACKSPACE.CO — Product Codes Master Record

All product codes across BACKSPACE.CO have been standardized to the official brand format:
`BSP-<CATEGORY>-<NUMBER>` (3-digit serial starting at `001` per category in current catalogue order).

All product objects retain an `oldCode` attribute for backwards compatibility, ensuring existing bookmarks, wishlist items, saved cart sessions, and search queries for legacy codes (`BKP...`) resolve seamlessly.
